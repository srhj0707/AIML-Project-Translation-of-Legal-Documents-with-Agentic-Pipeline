IN THE HIGH COURT OF JUDICATURE AT ALLAHABAD

CRIMINAL MISC. BAIL APPLICATION NO. 14205 OF 2023

Vikram Singh ... Applicant / Accused
Versus
State of Uttar Pradesh ... Respondent / Complainant

CORAM: HON'BLE MR. JUSTICE RAJESH KUMAR

ORDER:

1. Heard Sri Ramesh Chandra, Learned Counsel for the Applicant, and Sri A.K. Sharma, Learned Additional Government Advocate for the State of Uttar Pradesh.

2. This is an application under Section 439 of the Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 for grant of regular bail to the Applicant in Case Crime No. 342 of 2023 under Sections 420, 467, 468, and 471 of the Indian Penal Code, 1860 registered at Police Station Gomti Nagar, Lucknow.

3. Learned Counsel for the Applicant submits that the Applicant is innocent and has been falsely implicated in the present case due to business rivalry. It is submitted that the dispute is essentially of a civil nature and no prima facie case is made out against the Applicant. The Applicant has been languishing in jail since 12th August, 2023.

4. Learned Additional Government Advocate vehemently opposed the bail application, contending that there are serious allegations of financial fraud involving Rs. 45,00,000/- (Rupees Forty Five Lakhs only) against the Applicant.

5. Having heard the parties and perused the record, without expressing any opinion on the merits of the case, this Court finds that the charge-sheet has already been submitted and the trial is likely to take considerable time.

6. Let the Applicant, Vikram Singh, be released on bail on his furnishing a personal bond in the sum of Rs. 1,00,000/- (Rupees One Lakh) with two reliable sureties each in the like amount to the satisfaction of the Learned Chief Judicial Magistrate, Lucknow, subject to the following conditions:
(i) The Applicant shall not tamper with the prosecution witnesses or intimidate any person acquainted with the facts of the case.
(ii) The Applicant shall cooperate with the trial and shall attend the Learned Trial Court on each date fixed unless exempted.
(iii) The Applicant shall not leave the territory of India without prior permission of the Trial Court.

7. In case of breach of any of the above conditions, the Learned Trial Court shall be at liberty to cancel the bail in accordance with law.

8. The Bail Application is accordingly allowed.
