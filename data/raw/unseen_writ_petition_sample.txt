MEMORANDUM OF WRIT PETITION UNDER ARTICLE 226 OF THE CONSTITUTION OF INDIA

The Petitioner above named respectfully submits as under: -

1- That the Petitioner is a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, engaged in civil construction and infrastructure development.

2- That Respondent No. 2 issued a tender notification dated 15 February 2026 for construction of a highway bypass involving an estimated contract value of ₹45,00,00,000/- (Rupees Forty-Five Crores only).

3- That the Petitioner emerged as the lowest bidder (L1) and deposited an Earnest Money Deposit (EMD) of ₹90,00,000/-.

4- That despite compliance with all tender stipulations, Respondent No. 2 arbitrarily cancelled the tender process on 05 October 2026 without issuing any show-cause notice.

5- That the impugned cancellation violates the principles of natural justice and Article 14 of the Constitution of India.

PRAYER:
Wherefore, the Petitioner respectfully prays that this Hon'ble Court may be pleased to:
(a) Issue a Writ of Certiorari quashing the impugned cancellation order dated 05 October 2026.
(b) Issue a Writ of Mandamus directing Respondent No. 2 to finalize the contract with the Petitioner.
(c) Grant interim stay of any fresh tender notification pending disposal of this Writ Petition.
