SETTLEMENT AWARD UNDER SECTION 21 OF THE LEGAL SERVICES AUTHORITIES ACT, 1987

The dispute between the Claimant and the Respondent Insurance Company has been referred to this National Lok Adalat for amicable settlement: -

1. The Claimant instituted the claim petition seeking statutory compensation on account of the untimely demise of her husband in a motor vehicle accident on 14 November 2024.

2. Both parties along with their Learned Counsels have appeared before the Lok Adalat Bench and held mutual negotiations in good faith.

3. The Respondent Insurance Company has agreed to pay a full and final consolidated settlement sum of ₹18,50,000/- (Rupees Eighteen Lakh Fifty Thousand only) in complete satisfaction of all claims.

4. The said compensation amount of ₹18,50,000/- shall be deposited by the Respondent before the Tribunal within 30 days from today. Failing which, the said amount shall carry interest at 9% per annum from the date of default until realization.

5. The Claimant accepts the said settlement voluntarily without any coercion, undue influence, or misrepresentation.

AWARD:
The award is passed in terms of the above joint memo of settlement. Court fee paid shall be refunded to the Claimant in accordance with Section 16 of the Court Fees Act, 1870.
This award shall be final and binding on all parties and no appeal shall lie against this award.
